Legend of the Glowing Blade
👑 You are the KING of the village! 👑

⚔ HOW TO PLAY ⚔

👑 You are the King of the village — look for the crown on your head in third person (V)! Shadow creatures are attacking! Protect your five dojos (the big red one plus the Jade, Azure, Amber and Violet dojos — each has training dummies), command your loyal soldiers, survive 5 waves and defeat the boss, Shogun Gloom. Build walls, practise on the training dummies, and grab coins and hearts.

👑 A Royal Quest panel on the left is a way to win, then keep playing. Walk to the village, talk to the King and Queen, and attend a wedding or ring the bell for a vote. Survival players also clear 5 waves; Peaceful and Creative can win the Peaceful Crown without fighting. Scroll down for the full steps.
🧭 Explore the huge world — forests, bamboo groves, deserts, snowy mountains and lakes. Find 💎 shrines (full heal + Spirit Burst), raid 🔺 shadow camps, and hunt 🧰 treasure chests (click a chest to open it). The red dot on the map always points home to the dojo.

⚔️ Survival, ☮️ Peaceful and 🎨 Creative (buttons here or in Pause): in Survival, night critters fight and waves bring extras. In Peaceful and Creative they still appear at night but do not attack. Left click breaks blocks; shrines and treasure chests still work.

🏘️ Oakwood Village is the big plains village farther east, between the Azure and Amber side dojos — follow the east path past the dojo line to the bell. Five-floor houses, a little castle, restaurants and a train loop are there. Grown-ups and kids are one big family. Press C / 💬 to talk, or C at the family-tree board by the bell. Villagers keep their houses forever and cannot be hurt. The village king and queen wear small crowns — yours is the biggest, and you are the true High King.

🛏️ Beds & furniture: search "bed", "sink" or "counter" (🔍). Each one is placed in one go and picked up whole. Right-click a bed (📱 aim + tap 🧱) to set your respawn point — at night you sleep until morning! Put counters side by side to build a kitchen (the sink is just for show — no running water).

🌍 Worlds: PLAY continues your last world. Your builds and progress save automatically (every 30s and when you pause) — or press 💾 Save in Pause. ✨ New World makes a fresh world and keeps your old ones. 🔒 Password encrypts a world's save on this device (no way to recover a forgotten password). Saves live in this browser only — 📤 Export a backup file to keep them safe.

👑 Royal Quest (a way to win, then keep playing): 1) walk to Oakwood Village, east of the side dojos, and talk to the village King and Queen; 2) attend a wedding, or ring the bell for a vote; 3) in Survival, clear 5 bad-guy waves (villagers help you). In Peaceful or Creative you can win the Peaceful Crown after steps 1 and 2. A big “YOU WIN” screen appears, then you can keep exploring.

🌍 Worlds are saved in this browser on this device only. A new player starts in a fresh world. Your other worlds on another device stay there.

Tip: slash the glowing orbs to send them back! On a tablet, use the left side to move and the right side to look; 🔍 opens search.
